Your engine is not the limiter in this role — reading the play is.

2
Keep a near-max stride in the warm-up even on a game like this one.
You topped out at 24.8 km/h, 84% of your best — you did not go to the well today. But you have hit 98% in a match, so any given game can ask for it. The warm-up has to assume the hardest version of the day.
3
Look at where your sprints bunched — the map knows where, you know why.
4 of your 17 sprints came in 2 bunches less than a minute apart, the longest 2 back-to-back, typically 2 min 36 s between efforts. Bunching is exactly what pressing looks like, so this is not a fault — it is a question: were those the press, or chasing balls that were gone? You will remember.
