Your engine is not the limiter in this role — reading the play is.

Keep a near-max stride in the warm-up even on a game like this one.
You topped out at 24.1 km/h, 82% of your best — you did not go to the well today. But you have hit 98% in a match, so any given game can ask for it. The warm-up has to assume the hardest version of the day.
